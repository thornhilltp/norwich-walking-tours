# Plan: rank for walking tour and ghost walk searches, then switch the ads off (25 Oct 2026)

Today the ads cover three searches we don't win in Google's normal results:

| Search | Google position now (28 days) | Covered by |
|---|---|---|
| norwich walking tour | 9.5 (was about 5 in early Oct) | Free tour ads, £3/day |
| norwich walking tours | 14.5 (was about 6) | Free tour ads |
| ghost walk norwich / norwich ghost walk | about 38, or not showing | Dark History ads, £8/day |

## Why we don't rank

1. **No page says "Norwich walking tour(s)" where Google looks hardest.**
   - The free tour page's title is "Free Walking Tour Norwich…".
   - Its main heading is "England's best-kept medieval city."
   - The exact phrase "Norwich walking tour" appears nowhere on the site.
2. **"Walking tours" (plural) means "show me the options".** Google answers it with list pages (TripAdvisor, GetYourGuide, VisitNorwich). We have no page listing all our walks: `/tours` is switched off on the live site.
3. **The free tour just moved address (19 Oct).** Google is still showing the old addresses. That takes 2 to 4 weeks to settle and can wobble rankings while it does.
4. **Ghost walks:** the Dark History page mentions "ghost walk" twice. It is only live for a few weeks a year, and the long-running ghost walks have years of links and reviews behind them.

## The plan

### Phase 1: let the move settle (now to about 16 Nov)
- Point every outside link at `/tours/free-walking-tour`: Google Business Profile, VisitNorwich, GuruWalk (also fix its missing `https://`), FreeTour.com, social bios.
- In Search Console, ask Google to index `/tours/free-walking-tour`.
- Change no words on the free tour page yet. Two changes at once make it impossible to tell which one worked.
- Ads: free tour stays at £3/day.

### Phase 2: give Google the words (from about 16 Nov)
- **Free tour page title:** work "Norwich walking tour" into it, e.g. "Norwich Walking Tour | Free, 2 Hours of History and Local Tips". Main heading stays as it is. Add one plain line near the top using the phrase, plus one FAQ: "What's the best walking tour in Norwich?" (already on our approved list).
- **Build `/tours`, titled "Norwich Walking Tours":** every walk we run (free tour, Dark History, private), one card each, honest facts, links to each page. This is the page for the plural search. Add it to the menu, footer and sitemap, and use the planned "Home › Tours › …" breadcrumb.
- **Internal links:** the homepage, the free things page and the where to stay page link to `/tours` with the words "Norwich walking tours", and to the free tour page with "Norwich walking tour".

### Phase 3: earn trust (ongoing, monthly)
- **Reviews:** keep the end-of-tour QR flowing. Review count and recency feed the Google Maps results that sit above the normal ones.
- **Links from Norwich sites:** hotel and B&B concierge sheet (to-do M9), Norwich BID / Visit Norwich "what's on", local press for Dark History, Norfolk bloggers. Five good local links beat any amount of on-page tweaking.
- **Google Business Profile:** a weekly post, fresh photos, and "walking tour" as a category if it isn't already.

### Phase 4: ghost walks (play the long game)
- **Keep the Dark History page live all year**, never deleted or moved. It already shows "New dates coming soon" when there are none. Rankings take months to build, so it has to be indexed long before Halloween 2027.
- **Answer the search honestly:** add a short section "Looking for a ghost walk in Norwich?" Ours is true history, not ghost stories, and here's what that's like. This matches what people type without pretending to be a ghost walk. Keep the page's existing "not a ghost walk" positioning.
- **Each run:** local press and VisitNorwich listings link to that page, building up year on year.

## When each ad comes off

Per search, not all at once. Check it in the Monday SEO report.

1. **Trigger:** Google position 3 or better for that search, 4 weeks running.
2. **Step down:** halve that keyword's bid cap (or the budget, if it's the campaign's only keyword) for 2 weeks.
3. **Check real bookings from Google** (search + ads together, from the booking database):
   - Within 20% of before: pause that keyword.
   - Down more than 20%: put the ad back. The ranking isn't doing the job yet.
4. **Re-check after 4 more weeks.** If the ranking slips back below 5, turn the keyword back on.

Likely timing:
- "norwich walking tour": January to February 2027, if Phase 2 lands in November.
- "norwich walking tours" (plural): later, because list sites hold the top spots. The ad may stay as a small top-up.
- Ghost walks: no sooner than autumn 2027. Run Dark History ads for each run until then. They cost £1.70 a guest, so it's cheap cover.

**One rule regardless of rankings:** don't advertise a full walk. If upcoming walks for a tour are sold out, pause its ads until new dates go on sale.
