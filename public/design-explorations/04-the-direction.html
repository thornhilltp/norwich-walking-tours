What you came for

The bits of Norwich you only get from someone who lives here.

Every place on the route has a Sandemans version of the story. Tom doesn't tell those. He tells you where his neighbour drinks, where the parish records contradict the plaque, and which alley nobody photographs.

Where to eat

Why Elm Hill
survived the Blitz.

One bloke. One bucket. One staircase. The official record gives the credit to the fire service. The unofficial one, which my neighbour got from her great-aunt, is better.

Pick a date

Free to book. Tip what it was worth.

Tours leave The Forum at 11. Fifteen people max. If a date is full, the next one is usually tomorrow.

  • No card needed Booking holds your spot. Pay at the end if you liked it.
  • £10 to £20 a head That's what people have tipped, on average. No pressure.
  • Change or cancel anytime Up to an hour before. I'd rather know.

"Pay-what-you-want" sounds awkward. Call it merit-based. If Tom earns it, you tip. If he doesn't, you walk off. Honest deal.
